New CombinedTuningModel reads CombinedTuning resources in both the binary and the XML encoding Maxis ships, exposes the merged document as XML, encodes it as XML the way Maxis writes it, and produces the individual tuning documents it contains with GetTunings.
New GetCombinedTuning and GetCombinedTuningAsync get a resource as a CombinedTuningModel.
