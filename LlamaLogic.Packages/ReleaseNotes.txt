ModFileManifestModel now treats a manifest field whose value cannot be parsed (a URL, a hash, or a resource key, including those of required mods) as absent and skips such list entries, instead of throwing out of GetModFileManifest, GetModFileManifestAndKey, GetModFileManifests, and their asynchronous counterparts.
ModFileManifestModel.TryParse now returns false for malformed YAML instead of throwing YamlException, and a YAML hash that is not valid hexadecimal is treated as absent.
ModFileManifestModel now reads every entry of a manifest list written without whitespace between its entries (previously it kept every other entry, or rejected the manifest when the list had one entry), and accepts empty lists.
