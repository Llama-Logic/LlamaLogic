LlamaLogic.Packages no longer depends on SixLabors.ImageSharp, whose 3.x line has unpatched security advisories and whose patched versions require a commercial license: DDS conversion now uses BCnEncoder.Net directly, PNG and JPEG decoding uses StbImageSharp, JPEG encoding uses StbImageWriteSharp, and PNGs are written by LlamaLogic.Packages itself.
DDS conversions produce the same pixels and the same DST bytes as before, and translucent JPEG alpha round-trips exactly; translucent JPEGs are now written at quality 100 without chroma subsampling, so they are closer to the source and somewhat larger.
The image conversion methods now throw InvalidDataException when given something other than a PNG (or, inside a translucent JPEG, an ALFA payload other than a PNG), an image that cannot be decoded, or an image with no pixels or more than 67,108,864 (8,192 by 8,192) pixels, rather than ImageSharp's exceptions or running out of memory; passing something other than a JPEG to the translucent JPEG readers still throws FormatException.
ConvertPngToTranslucentJpegAsync and ConvertTranslucentJpegToPngAsync now do their work on the thread pool.
