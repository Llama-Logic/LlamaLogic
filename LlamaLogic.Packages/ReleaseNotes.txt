Breaking: DataModel and its types are removed, along with GetData and GetDataAsync; SimDataType and SimDataVersion replace DataModelType and DataModelVersion.
New SimDataModel reads SimData resources as named instances of objects described by schemas, with vectors and variants as SimDataVector and SimDataVariant, validates values as they are set, and encodes resources the way Maxis does, reproducing the game's own SimData byte for byte.
New GetSimData and GetSimDataAsync get a resource as a SimDataModel.
Binary CombinedTuning resources are no longer given the name "documents" when resource names are indexed.
