SetPngAsTranslucentJpeg, SetPngAsTranslucentJpegAsync, ConvertPngToTranslucentJpeg, and ConvertPngToTranslucentJpegAsync now throw InvalidDataException when the image's alpha channel compresses to more than 65,525 bytes, which a translucent JPEG cannot hold, instead of silently writing a corrupt JPEG.
GetDdsAsPng, GetDdsAsPngAsync, GetPngDataFromDdsData, and GetPngDataFromDdsDataAsync now throw InvalidDataException for a DirectDraw Surface that declares more than 32 mipmap levels, instead of running out of memory.
GetDdsAsPngAsync now passes its cancellation token to the conversion.
