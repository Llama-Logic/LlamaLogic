Remarks

Hypothesis: it identifies a wall edge, its bytes packing the level (high nibble of byte 0, signed) and a kind (low nibble), a direction (byte 1: 0, 1, 6 or 7) and two coordinates (bytes 2 and 3). About 42% of the keys in the lots examined are also Id values of the bundle's VertexGraphsSection.

Remarks
