A whole number in the lots examined. Hypothesis: a coordinate. In each of the 252 entries examined in lots that also hold a LegacyWallGraphSection, it, Unknown3 and Unknown1 equal bits 7 through 13, bits 0 through 6 and bits 28 through 31 of a Code or Code of that section (or bits 21 through 27, bits 14 through 20 and bits 28 through 31); the other 80 entries are in lots without that section.
