Provides methods for dealing with sounds in Electronic Arts' AudioCore format: the AudioVocals resources of The Sims 4 (a header, followed by its audio if the sound is held in memory) and their AudioEffects bodies (the audio of a streamed sound)

Remarks

The rebuilding of EALayer3 into MP3 is adapted from vgmstream (see Formats/Vgmstream/license.txt); unlike vgmstream, it lays the audio out at a constant bitrate with a bit reservoir and adds a gapless playback tag, so that ordinary MP3 decoders play it sample for sample as the game does

Remarks

The sound's length comes from the MP3's gapless playback tag (as LAME and the tools built on it write); an MP3 without one is taken to end where its last frame does. The game's sounds begin with 47 samples stored uncompressed, which this writes as silence, so the first millisecond of the MP3 is not heard.

Exceptions

InvalidDataException

The MP3 is malformed, or its tag declares an encoder delay of less than 576 samples

NotSupportedException

The MP3 is not MPEG-1 Layer III (32,000, 44,100 or 48,000 Hz), or is free format
