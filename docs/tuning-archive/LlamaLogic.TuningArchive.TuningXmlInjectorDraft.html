Remarks

XML Injector can only add: interactions to objects, to the Sim object, to phones and to the relationship panel; interactions to affordance lists; loot actions to loot actions and random weighted loot; buffs to traits; states and state triggers to objects; name and object relationship components to objects that have none; and purchase list options and picker categories to purchase picker interactions. Any other change, and any removal, is reported as an error and left out.
